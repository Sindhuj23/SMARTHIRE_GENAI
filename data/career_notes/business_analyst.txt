ROLE GUIDE: BUSINESS ANALYST

Overview:
A Business Analyst bridges the gap between business needs and technical solutions. The role focuses more on requirement gathering, process improvement, and stakeholder communication than on deep coding.

Core Skills Required:
- Requirement gathering and documentation (writing clear user stories/specs)
- Basic SQL and Excel for data checks, not heavy engineering
- Process mapping tools (flowcharts, BPMN basics)
- Strong verbal and written communication skills
- Familiarity with Agile/Scrum ways of working
- Tools: Jira, Confluence, PowerPoint for presenting findings

How to Switch Into This Role:
1. Practice writing clear requirement documents and user stories for a sample product idea.
2. Learn basic SQL and Excel — enough to validate data, not build pipelines.
3. Get comfortable presenting a problem, options, and a recommendation in a short deck.
4. Highlight any past experience coordinating between teams, even informally, on your resume.

Common Interview Topics:
- "Tell me about a time you gathered requirements from a stakeholder with unclear needs."
- Case studies on improving a business process
- Basic SQL query questions
- Questions on prioritization frameworks (e.g. MoSCoW)

Typical Weak Points for Beginners:
- Too technical in interviews when the interviewer wants a business-first answer
- Vague requirement documents without measurable acceptance criteria
- Not asking clarifying questions before jumping to solutions

Related Roles: Product Analyst, Product Owner, Data Analyst, Project Coordinator
